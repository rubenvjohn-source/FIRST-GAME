PIXEL BALLER - Basketball Player Sprite Pack
by Eric Lamb

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 7 animations, 34 frames total, each frame 80x140 px (transparent PNG)
    dribble      6 frames  (10 fps suggested)
    jumpshot     9 frames  (12 fps)
    run          6 frames  (12 fps)
    run_dribble  6 frames  (12 fps)
    defense      2 frames  (4 fps)
    pass         3 frames  (10 fps)
    idle         2 frames  (3 fps)
- 18 colorways: 6 uniforms (orange/purple, blue/gold, red/black, green/white,
  white/navy, black/red) x 3 skin tones (light, medium, dark)
- sheets/   full sprite sheet per colorway, 1x (720x980) and 4x (2880x3920)
- strips/   one horizontal strip per animation per colorway
- previews/ animated GIF previews

SHEET LAYOUT (1x)
Grid of 80x140 cells, 9 columns. Row order top to bottom:
dribble, jumpshot, run, run_dribble, defense, pass, idle.
Rows with fewer than 9 frames are padded with empty cells.
Jersey chest is left blank so you can draw your own numbers in-engine.
Scale with nearest-neighbor filtering to keep pixels crisp.
